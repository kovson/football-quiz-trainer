# Football Quiz Preparation

The quiz team's shared vocabulary for preparing for Piłkarski Quiz competitions.

## Language

**Quiz team**:
The group of people who compete together in Piłkarski Quiz and prepare for its questions.
_Avoid_: Football team (which means a team of football players).

**Quiz event**:
A scheduled Piłkarski Quiz competition that the quiz team prepares for and attends.

**League quiz**:
Piłkarski Quiz's general-football event format, covering the organizer's top ten leagues, cups, national teams, players, clubs, stadiums, and other football topics.

**Recent-events window**:
The period between the quiz team's previous quiz event and its target quiz event, defining which football developments count as recent for preparation.
_Avoid_: Last four weeks (the window follows quiz events rather than a fixed duration).

**Training session**:
A dated set of football questions shared by all quiz-team members for that day's preparation. Each day has a new training session.

**Daily practice**:
A quiz-team member's attempt at a training session, completed at their own pace and optionally over several sittings.

**Question subject**:
The main player, coach, club, national team, or stadium whose identity or attributes a question tests. A multi-part question can have several subjects; names appearing only as background clues are not subjects.

**Subject cooldown**:
A minimum gap of thirty days, preferably sixty or more, before a question subject is the main focus of another new question. This also applies to recent-event questions; a country question about a player has that player as its subject.

**Question reuse**:
Publishing a previously used question, including one that merely changes its wording, in another daily training session. Returning to an archived session is a separate activity.

### Question formats

**Career riddle**:
A question asking the member to identify a footballer from a short written summary of their life and playing career.

**Shared coach**:
A question asking the member to identify a coach who coached each of the named players, potentially at different clubs or times.

**Club riddle**:
A question asking the member to identify a club from clues about its players, history, fortunes, or crest.

**Missing squad members**:
A question showing a squad from a specific historical match with some player names omitted for the member to identify.

**Crest identification**:
A question asking the member to name a club from its crest image or a description of its crest.

**Photo identification**:
A question asking the member to identify a player or coach from a photograph. Player identification uses the face without identifying club kit or badge clues.

**Shared club**:
A question asking which club named players were teammates at during overlapping spells.

**Transfer clues**:
A question asking the member to identify a player from a transfer's year, selling club, buying club, and reported fee.

**Transfer history**:
A question asking the member to identify a player from a sequence of club badges and dates in their career.

**Career gap**:
A question asking the member to fill a missing club in a named player’s career path, with the other stages shown as club badges and dates.

**Current-season question**:
A question about the ongoing season, with an explicit season or match date. It can concern a latest completed league match without falling inside the recent-events window.

**Stadium question**:
A question about a football stadium's identity or a distinctive feature or matchday fact associated with it.

**Country question**:
A question about a player's birthplace, nationality, or national team, explicitly stating which country relationship is being asked about.

**Kit identification**:
A question asking the member to identify a club, national team, or kit manufacturer from a shirt associated with an explicitly stated season or tournament.

**Match result question**:
A question about the score of a specified football match, stating whether the answer includes extra time or a penalty shoot-out.

**Football fact question**:
A question about a football record, competition, award, rule, or unusual fact, with an explicit date or season when the answer can change.

**True-or-false question**:
A question asking whether one or more clearly stated football claims are true, with an explanation for each claim.

### Answers and practice

**Answer part**:
One distinct answer within a question, such as a missing squad member or the player identified by one of several transfer clues.

**Answer reveal**:
The step in daily practice that shows all answer parts so the member can compare them with their own recollection.
